Razieh Nabi

I am a Rollins Assistant Professor in the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at Emory University. My research focuses on making reliable decisions from imperfect data through advances in causal inference, missing data methodology, and statistical learning. I received my PhD from Johns Hopkins University in May 2021, where I had the privilege of working with Ilya Shpitser.
