Teaching

Integrating research and teaching within an intellectual community is one of the most rewarding aspects of academia. I view research and teaching as deeply interconnected endeavors. Research enables me to bring current ideas and discoveries into the classroom, while teaching challenges me to clarify concepts, explore new perspectives, and continue growing as a scholar.

Teaching philosophy

"Zakaate elm dar nashre aan aast," which means the "taxes" on acquired knowledge are best paid by teaching it to others.

A Law of Iterated Expectation Primer for Causal Inference

Graduate-level courses

BIOS 760R

Advanced Causal Inference

Graduate-level course on advanced causal inference from observational data, emphasizing graphical models and semiparametric efficiency theory. Topics include nonparametric identification in the presence of unmeasured confounding in DAGs with hidden variabels, influence-function based estimation, and mediation analysis.

Recipient of the Departmental Distinguished Teaching Award.

Emory University Fall 2022 and 2023
Syllabus (PDF)

BIOS 761 / EPI 760

Causal Inference

Graduate-level course providing a comprehensive introduction to modern causal inference. Topics include potential outcomes, causal effect identification, graphical causal models, G-computation, inverse probability weighting, doubly robust estimation, and the integration of machine learning methods for causal effect estimation.

Emory University Spring 2025 and 2026

Causal infernece . Python · Applied workflow

Hands-On Causal Inference with Python

Practice-oriented virtual short course introducing modern causal inference for data scientists and machine learning practitioners. Participants learn how to formulate causal questions, assess identifiability using causal graphs, and implement principled estimation strategies in Python using observational data.

Short course on graphical approaches to nonignorable missing data, including representation, identification, sensitivity analysis, and practical strategies for empirical research.
